    HelloHost Documentation

    Welcome to HelloHost - the complete subscription management platform for your business.

    HelloHost helps you manage clients, automate billing, handle domains and hosting, track support tickets, and grow your business efficiently.

    1. Getting Started - Create your account and complete initial setup
    2. Configuration - Configure your settings, branding, and integrations

    What You Can Do With HelloHost

    Manage Clients

    • Store complete client information and billing details
    • Organize clients with labels and categories
    • Give clients access to a branded portal
    • Securely share passwords and important information
    • Track all interactions with activity logs

    Automate Billing

    • Create recurring subscriptions that bill automatically
    • Generate professional PDF invoices with your branding
    • Accept online payments with multiple payment methods
    • Send automatic payment reminders
    • Support multiple currencies and languages

    Handle Hosting & Domains

    • Connect to domain registrars (OXXA, Versio)
    • Automatically register and manage domains
    • Edit DNS zones directly from HelloHost
    • Connect hosting servers (cPanel, DirectAdmin)
    • Automatically provision hosting accounts

    Track Support & Tasks

    • Manage support tickets from clients
    • Assign tasks to team members
    • Set priorities and due dates
    • Track time spent on billable work
    • Convert tasks directly to invoice items

    Monitor Your Business

    • Track revenue and forecast future income
    • Monitor expenses and profitability
    • View payment history and reports
    • Track website performance for clients
    • See all activity in one dashboard

    Team Collaboration

    • Add team members with custom permissions
    • Control who can access financial data
    • Assign tasks and track completion
    • Review work before it's sent to clients
