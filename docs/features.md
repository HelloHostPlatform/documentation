# Features

A comprehensive list of all features offered by HelloHost.

## Client Management

* **Client Accounts** - Complete customer information management with billing details
* **Client Dashboard** - Dedicated portal for clients to manage their services
* **Account Management** - Self-service account settings and preferences
* **Labels** - Organize and categorize clients with custom labels and colors
* **Password Memos** - Securely store and share passwords with clients
* **Contacts** - Multiple users per client account with individual permissions
* **Activity Tracking** - Comprehensive audit logs of all client interactions
* **Client Search** - Quick search and filtering across all client data

## Service Management

* **Service Catalog** - Configurable service offerings (domains, hosting, custom services)
* **Service Categories** - Organize services into categories for better management
* **Service Types:**
  - Domain registration services
  - Shared hosting packages
  - Custom services for any billable item
* **Service Templates** - Pre-configured services for quick deployment
* **Pricing Management** - Flexible pricing with historical price tracking
* **Featured Services** - Highlight popular services for clients
* **Internal Services** - Mark services as internal-only (not visible to clients)

## Subscription Management

* **Various Subscription Types** - Support for domains, hosting, and custom services
* **Custom Pricing** - Individual pricing per subscription
* **Flexible Renewal Cycles** - Monthly, quarterly, yearly, or custom intervals
* **Auto-renewal Management** - Automated subscription renewals
* **Manual Renewal** - Option for manual renewal control
* **Subscription Notifications** - Notify clients before renewal
* **Subscription History** - Track all subscription changes and renewals
* **Cancellation Management** - Graceful subscription cancellation workflow

## Orders & E-commerce

* **Order System** - Shopping cart and order processing
* **Order Management** - Track and manage customer orders
* **Order Notifications** - Automated emails for new orders and payments
* **Order Invoicing** - Automatic invoice generation from orders
* **Payment Links** - Direct payment links via Mollie
* **Order Comments** - Internal notes and communication on orders

## Invoice & Payment Management

* **Automated Invoice Creation** - Generate invoices from subscriptions and orders
* **Invoice Reminders** - Scheduled reminders for unpaid invoices
* **Multi-currency Support** - Handle invoices in different currencies
* **VAT Module** - EU VAT validation and calculation
* **Tax Rate Configuration** - Customizable tax rates per invoice
* **Payment Processing** - Integration with Mollie for online payments
* **Direct Debit** - SEPA direct debit mandates and automatic collection
* **Payment Methods:**
  - iDEAL
  - Credit Card
  - SEPA Direct Debit
  - PayPal
  - Bancontact
  - Other Mollie-supported methods
* **Invoice PDF Generation** - Professional PDF invoices with branding
* **Payment History** - Complete payment tracking and reporting
* **Refunds** - Process full and partial refunds
* **Credits** - Apply account credits to invoices
* **Coupons** - Discount codes and promotional offers

## Hosting Management

* **Domain Registration** - Register new domains via integrated registrars
* **Domain Transfer** - Transfer domains from other providers
* **Domain Listing** - View and manage all registered domains
* **DNS Management** - Full DNS zone editing (A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, etc.)
* **Domain Locking** - Lock/unlock domains to prevent unauthorized transfers
* **Auth Codes** - Request EPP/auth codes for domain transfers
* **Auto-renewal Configuration** - Enable/disable domain auto-renewal
* **Domain Notifications** - Alerts for registration failures or transfers
* **Shared Hosting Accounts** - Create and manage hosting accounts
* **Server Integration:**
  - cPanel account creation and deletion
  - DirectAdmin account creation and deletion
* **Hosting Notifications** - Alerts for account creation failures

## Task & Ticket Management

* **Task System** - Comprehensive task and ticket management
* **Client Tickets** - Support tickets from clients
* **Internal Tasks** - Private tasks for team members
* **Task Priorities** - Low, Normal, High, Urgent priority levels
* **Task Status Tracking** - Open, In Progress, Completed, Closed states
* **Due Dates** - Set deadlines for tasks
* **Task Assignment** - Assign tasks to team members
* **Task Review** - Review workflow for completed tasks
* **Recurring Tasks** - Automated task generation on schedules
* **Task Templates** - Pre-defined task structures
* **Task Comments** - Discussion threads on tasks
* **Time Tracking** - Track time spent on tasks
* **Invoice Integration** - Add completed work to client invoices
* **Task Workflows** - Custom workflows for ticket processing
* **Attachments** - Upload files to tasks
* **Task Notifications** - Email notifications for task updates

## Content Management

* **Content Blocks** - Reusable content blocks for emails and pages
* **Multi-language Content** - Translate content blocks for different languages
* **Markdown Support** - Rich text formatting with Markdown
* **Content Templating** - Use content blocks in notifications and pages

## Statistics & Reporting

* **Revenue Statistics** - Track income over time
* **Revenue Forecasting** - Project future revenue based on subscriptions
* **Expense Tracking** - Record business expenses and costs
* **Expense Reports** - Calculate expense-to-revenue ratios
* **Subscription Analytics** - Active subscriptions and churn metrics
* **Payment Analytics** - Payment success rates and methods
* **Chart Visualizations** - Interactive charts and graphs
* **Custom Date Ranges** - Filter reports by specific time periods

## Performance Monitoring

* **PageSpeed Reports** - Website performance tracking via Google Lighthouse
* **Desktop & Mobile Scores** - Separate performance metrics
* **Historical Tracking** - Track performance changes over time
* **Client-specific Reports** - Performance reports per client website
* **Automated Syncing** - Regular performance data updates

## Security & Authentication

* **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)** - TOTP-based 2FA for enhanced security
* **Role-Based Access Control** - Granular permissions using Bouncer
* **User Roles:**
  - Super Admin
  - Admin
  - Employee (with customizable permissions)
  - Client
* **Activity Logs** - Comprehensive audit trails of all actions
* **Secure Password Storage** - Bcrypt/Argon2 password hashing
* **Login Links** - Secure passwordless authentication
* **Session Management** - Secure session handling
* **Account Activation** - Email verification for new accounts

## Permission Management

* **Granular Permissions** - Fine-grained access control for employees
* **Role Management** - Define custom roles with specific abilities
* **Client Management Permissions:**
  - View clients
  - Create/edit clients
  - Delete clients
  - Manage client subscriptions
  - Access client passwords
* **Financial Permissions:**
  - View invoices
  - Create/edit invoices
  - Process payments
  - Manage refunds
* **Task Permissions:**
  - View all tasks
  - Edit all tasks
  - Assign tasks
  - Review tasks
* **System Permissions:**
  - Manage settings
  - Manage users
  - Manage services
  - View statistics

* **Automatic Translation** - DeepL integration for content translation
* **Localized Invoices** - Invoices in client's preferred language
* **Localized Notifications** - Emails in recipient's language
